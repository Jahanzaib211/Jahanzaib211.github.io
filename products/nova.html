NOVA

What if you could see how an AI agent does its work?

The problem

An AI agent can produce a convincing answer while leaving its process hidden. It is then hard to judge the result, correct its course or learn why something failed.

The thinking

Trust grows when work can be inspected. A professional shows drafts, tests and decisions. We wanted an agent held to the same standard.

The solution we designed

NOVA gives the agent a computer of its own, presented through six views. Sub-agents divide complex work, memory preserves context, and each conversation has its own sandbox.

What guides it

  • Work is visible while it happens
  • Each conversation is contained
  • Results arrive with evidence to review
  • Open-source foundations are credited in the source
