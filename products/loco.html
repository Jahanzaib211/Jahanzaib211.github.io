LOCO

What if local AI told you the truth about its limits?

The problem

Running models on your own hardware often means several separate servers, memory that runs out without warning, and one model quietly standing in for another.

The thinking

Predictability is a feature. A system that declines with clear numbers is more useful than one that accepts a request and fails. Control also means deciding what an assistant may remember.

The solution we designed

LOCO places local models behind one endpoint. It measures available GPU memory before loading a model, follows deterministic routing, and proposes lasting memories for your approval.

What guides it

  • Refuse with numbers rather than fail silently
  • Never substitute a model in secret
  • Keep memory under human approval
  • Open source under Apache 2.0
