STORELOOM

What if a storefront could grow without losing its shape?

The problem

Selling online brings together design, products, payments, domains and daily operations. As a brand grows, these pieces spread across tools that were never designed as one system.

The thinking

We studied commerce as an operating problem. Each brand needs freedom to express itself, and firm separation so that its data and files remain its own.

The solution we designed

STORELOOM brings templates, a visual editor, apps, catalogs and orders into one platform. Each site is designed with its own database and file store.

What guides it

  • One system for design and operation
  • Separate data and files for each site
  • Flexible blocks within a consistent structure
  • Built as an original ALI LABS X product
