NOVA BOTS

What if AI worked like a well-run team?

The problem

Most AI assistants are a single chat window. They answer, then lose the context. Real work needs clear roles, continuity, and people who can hand a task to the right colleague.

The thinking

We asked what makes a good team dependable. The answer was structure. Defined responsibilities, shared conversation, review before delivery, and one person who stays in charge.

The solution we designed

NOVA BOTS gives each bot a role, instructions, a model, memory and its own working folder. Bots form squads and pass work along a chain, while Orion coordinates the effort. People approve what matters.

What guides it

  • Every bot has one clear responsibility
  • Work is reviewed before it is delivered
  • Access is denied when a check cannot be completed
  • A person makes the final decision
