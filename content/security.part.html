Security

What protects this website and your enquiry, and how to tell us about a weakness.

This website

  • Pages are static files served over an encrypted connection.
  • There are no accounts, advertising or analytics.
  • The public form can create an enquiry and cannot read one.
  • Enquiries are kept on our own system and reading them requires a signed in console.

Client systems

Controls for a client’s system are agreed in writing for that system, because the right ones depend on the data and where it runs. We do not claim a certification we do not hold.

Report a concern

Write to Jahanzaib@alilabsx.com with the address or product, the steps to reproduce it and the effect you expect. Please do not include other people’s data.

Please test gently. Do not disrupt a service, open data that is not yours, use social engineering or publish the weakness before we have had a fair chance to repair it. If you act in good faith and follow this page, we will work with you to understand and fix the matter. Testing a client’s system needs that client’s permission.
