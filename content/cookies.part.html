Cookies

One cookie remembers your choice. Analytics, including Google Analytics, stays off unless you accept.

The cookie we set

The cookie alx_consent remembers whether you accepted or rejected analytics. It lasts up to 180 days and holds only your answer. You can change your answer at any time.

If you sign in to the client space, our own system sets the cookie __Secure-alx_client. It keeps you signed in, lasts up to 12 hours, is needed for signing in, and is not used for counting visits.

What happens if you accept

Our own system counts page views, the site you came from, language, device type, and actions such as opening or sending the form. It does not record what you type. To count daily visitors, your IP address and browser name are turned into a code with a secret that is kept in memory and replaced each day. The code is not stored with your IP address, and codes from earlier days are deleted when the next day begins to be counted. If your browser sends a Global Privacy Control signal, we treat it as a rejection.

Google Analytics also loads. It sets the cookies _ga and _ga_612W223T3P, which last up to 180 days, and receives the page address, the site you came from, your approximate location, your device and browser, and actions such as scrolling, following a link or opening the form. Google receives your IP address when its script loads. We turn off advertising features and do not send what you type in the form. Google handles this data under its own terms and may process it outside your country. If you reject later, we stop Google Analytics on this site and delete these cookies from your browser.

What is stored in your browser

Using session storage, the site notes which help messages you have already seen, whether you opened the discovery form, and whether the page reloaded itself after an update. The notes contain nothing about you and disappear when you close the tab. What you type in the discovery form stays in the open page until you send or leave.

Services that deliver the site

Our hosting and network providers process technical data such as IP addresses to deliver pages and protect them. If you follow a link to another site, such as WhatsApp, GitHub or LinkedIn, that site applies its own cookie rules.
