What we collect

This site has no accounts or advertising. If you accept, we count visits on our own system, as explained on our cookies page. One cookie remembers your answer. Visits are sent through Cloudflare to our system in Pakistan. It notes in your browser, until you close the tab, which help messages you have already seen, whether you opened the discovery form, and whether the page reloaded itself after an update. The discovery form asks about your problem, your project and how to contact you. When you send it, the brief travels through Cloudflare to our own system in Pakistan. If our system does not answer, the form offers you an email to send instead. Our form wording is also loaded from that system. We use what you send to answer you, calculate a fit score that sets the order in which we reply, prepare proposals and keep business records. A person reads every brief, and no decision about you is made automatically. You can ask us to erase it. Daily backups keep up to 14 copies.

If you choose to contact us on WhatsApp, the link opens WhatsApp with a short greeting. Nothing is sent until you press send. WhatsApp then handles the conversation under its own privacy policy.

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical data such as IP addresses for security and operation. See GitHub's privacy statement.

Sharing and keeping data

We do not sell personal data. Email and hosting providers process data for us, possibly outside your country. We keep enquiries only as long as needed for the discussion, contracts, claims or legal duties.
