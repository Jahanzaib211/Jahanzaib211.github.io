About

I build production-grade systems solo — end to end. That means the AI agent runtime and its orchestration layer, the LLM gateway it talks to, the database and infra underneath, and the hosting stack that keeps it all online. Not prototypes — systems with admin control planes, observability, budgets, and audit trails from day one.

My work spans autonomous computer-use agents, self-hosted LLM routing and proxy infrastructure, fintech platforms handling real transactions, and the hosting/licensing systems that sit under all of it. I move fluently across the stack — Python and Rust on the backend, Next.js and NestJS on the front, Postgres and Docker everywhere in between — because shipping a real product means owning every layer, not just the interesting one.
