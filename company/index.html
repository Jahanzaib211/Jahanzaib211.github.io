You depend on it every day. Do you own it?

Consider the one system your organization could least afford to lose.

  • If its price doubled, would you have a choice?
  • If access ended, would your work continue?
  • If you were asked where your data resides, could you answer with certainty?

Our founder put these questions to his own work, and chose to own the answers. That choice still guides everything we engineer.

Four problems. Four products. One standard of ownership.

  • NOVA BOTS brings structure and accountability to teams of AI.
  • NOVA makes an agent’s work visible, so it can be reviewed before it is trusted.
  • LOCO runs intelligence on your own hardware and is clear about its limits.
  • STORELOOM lets a business grow its store and keep control of it.

A company built on established work.
