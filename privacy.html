Privacy policy

Last updated 23 November 2026

Who and what this covers

ALI LABS X (PRIVATE) LIMITED, CUIN 0366968, operates alilabsx.com. Our products and client projects may have separate notices or agreements.

What we collect

This site has no accounts, advertising or analytics, and it does not set cookies. It notes in your browser, until you close the tab, which help messages you have already seen. The discovery form asks about your problem, your project and how to contact you. When you send it, the brief travels through Cloudflare to our own system in Pakistan. If our system does not answer, the form offers you an email to send instead. Our form wording is also loaded from that system. We use what you send to answer you, calculate a fit score that sets the order in which we reply, prepare proposals and keep business records. A person reads every brief, and no decision about you is made automatically. You can ask us to erase it. Daily backups keep up to 14 copies.

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical data such as IP addresses for security and operation. See GitHub's privacy statement.

Sharing and keeping data

We do not sell personal data. Email and hosting providers process data for us, possibly outside your country. We keep enquiries only as long as needed for the discussion, contracts, claims or legal duties.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may ask to access, correct, delete, restrict, move or object to use of your data, and complain to your data protection authority. Email Jahanzaib@alilabsx.com. We may need to verify your identity.

Links and changes

Linked sites have their own policies. We will post changes here with a new date. This site is for businesses and is not directed at children.
